# Contributing

The project source is licensed under MIT; third-party components retain their own terms. Before proposing algorithm changes,
record units, coordinate spaces, calibration/data provenance and expected numerical effects.
Run `python tests/run_tests.py`, `python examples/precision_demo.py` and
`python scripts/check_project.py`. Do not contribute patient data, device identifiers,
vendor binaries/definitions, credentials or third-party assets without redistribution evidence.
Contributions must be compatible with MIT and preserve applicable third-party notices.
